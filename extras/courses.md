# 📚 Top 5 Free Philosophy Courses

---

## 1. [**Justice — Harvard University (HarvardX)**](https://www.edx.org/learn/justice/harvard-university-justice)
**Instructor**: Michael Sandel — world-renowned political philosopher.  
**Platform**: edX  
**Focus**: Moral reasoning, ethics, political philosophy, civic duties.  
**Format**: Video lectures, readings, case studies.

---

## 2. [**Philosophy and Critical Thinking — University of Queensland (UQx)**](https://www.edx.org/learn/humanities/the-university-of-queensland-philosophy-and-critical-thinking-5)
**Platform**: edX  
**Focus**: Argument structure, logical reasoning, introduction to philosophical inquiry.  
**Length**: ~4 weeks, 1–4 hours/week.  
**Format**: Short MOOC, videos, quizzes.

---

## 3. [**Introduction to Western Philosophy — Hillsdale College**](https://online.hillsdale.edu/courses/promo/introduction-to-western-philosophy)
**Platform**: Hillsdale Free Online Courses  
**Focus**: Major Western thinkers — Plato, Aristotle, Aquinas, Descartes, Nietzsche.  
**Format**: 14 video lectures, readings, quizzes.

---

## 4. [**Open Yale Courses — “Death” & “Philosophy and the Science of Human Nature”**](https://oyc.yale.edu/philosophy)
**Platform**: Open Yale Courses  
**Focus**:  
- *Death* (Shelly Kagan): Philosophical questions around death and immortality.  
- *Philosophy and the Science of Human Nature* (Tamar Gendler): Human nature, moral psychology.  
**Format**: Full lecture videos, reading lists, transcripts.

---

## 5. [**MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW) — Philosophy**](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/ocwsearch.htm?q=philosophy)
**Platform**: MIT OCW  
**Focus**: Variety — metaphysics, philosophy of mind, ethics, logic.  
**Format**: Lecture notes, problem sets, exams (some with video/audio archives).
