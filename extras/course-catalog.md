## 📝 How to Use This Catalog

1. **Browse by Category**: Courses are organized by philosophical subdiscipline
2. **Filter by Difficulty**: Use the dropdown menu to find courses matching your experience level
3. **Search**: Use the search box to find specific topics, instructors, or platforms
4. **Enroll**: Click the "Enroll Now" or "Access Course" buttons to get started

## 💡 Tips for Success

- **Start with beginner courses** if you're new to philosophy
- **Take notes** and engage with course materials actively
- **Join discussion forums** to connect with other learners
- **Read primary sources** when possible, not just summaries
