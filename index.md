# Open Source Philosophy Degree

Welcome to the **Open Source Philosophy Degree** - your gateway to free, high-quality philosophy education from the world's leading universities and institutions.

## What is This Project?

This is a curated collection of free, open-access philosophy courses and resources designed to provide a comprehensive philosophical education without the cost of traditional university programs. Whether you're a curious beginner or a seasoned philosophy enthusiast, you'll find structured learning paths and exceptional courses here.

## Why Study Philosophy?

Philosophy develops critical thinking, logical reasoning, and the ability to analyze complex problems - skills that are valuable in any career. It explores fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, ethics, and the human condition.

## Features

- **Completely Free** - All courses are freely accessible
- **High Quality** - From Harvard, MIT, Yale, Oxford, and other top institutions
- **Self-Paced** - Learn on your own schedule
- **Comprehensive** - Covers all major areas of philosophy
- **Structured Paths** - Organized learning trajectories from beginner to advanced

## Get Started

1. **New to Philosophy?** Start with our [Top 5 Courses](/courses/) or check the Beginner's Path in our [Help Guide](/help/)
2. **Have Specific Interests?** Browse the [Course Catalog](/course-catalog/) and filter by category
3. **Want to Go Deep?** Review our [Curricular Guidelines](/curricular-guidelines/) for a comprehensive 4-year plan

## Course Categories

- **Ethics & Moral Philosophy** - Questions of right and wrong, virtue, justice
- **Logic & Reasoning** - Principles of valid inference and argumentation
- **Metaphysics** - Nature of reality, existence, causation, free will
- **Epistemology** - Theory of knowledge, skepticism, justification
- **Political Philosophy** - Justice, rights, government, social structures
- **Philosophy of Mind** - Consciousness, mental states, mind-body problem
- **History of Philosophy** - Ancient, Medieval, Modern, and Contemporary thought

## About This Project

This is a community-driven initiative to make quality philosophy education accessible to everyone. We believe that philosophical thinking should be available to all, regardless of economic circumstances.
